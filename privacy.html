Delta Technologies (K. Dimitriou 5, Spata 190 04, Greece — VAT 803386643, ΔΟΥ ΚΕΦΟΔΕ Αττικής) respects your privacy. This page explains what personal data we handle through this website and how we treat it.

What this website collects

This site is a static page. It has no contact form, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising or tracking cookies. We do not profile visitors.

Our hosting provider processes standard technical request data (IP address, browser type, time of request) for security and availability. The web font used on this page is loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of that request.

If you contact us

When you email us, we receive the address you write from and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to answer you and to manage the business relationship that may follow. We keep this correspondence for as long as it is relevant, and no longer than legal record-keeping obligations require.

Legal basis

We process this data under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract) and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in operating a secure website and responding to enquiries).

Sharing

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with third parties for marketing. Data may be processed by service providers acting on our instructions — hosting and email providers — under appropriate agreements.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request:

  • access to the personal data we hold about you
  • correction of inaccurate data
  • erasure of your data, where applicable
  • restriction of, or objection to, processing
  • a copy of your data in a portable format

Write to info@mydrive.gr and we will respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

Customers of our products

Data processed inside our software products on behalf of our customers is covered by the agreement with that customer, where we act as data processor. This policy covers only this website.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
